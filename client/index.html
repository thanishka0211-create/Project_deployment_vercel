About VU-REC

The Research Ethics Committee has been constituted by Vidyashilp University to oversee research involving human participants in accordance with ICMR and UGC ethical guidelines.

Objectives

  • Protect research participants.
  • Review research proposals ethically.
  • Ensure informed consent.
  • Maintain participant privacy and confidentiality.
  • Promote responsible research practices.
